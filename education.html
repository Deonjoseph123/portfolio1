INDIAN LEARNERS OWN ACADEMY

(2010-2024)

This is the school that I have studied in for the past 14 years

Indian Learners Own Academy came into existence on 1st September 2010 with the objective of encouraging students to aim for excellence and to fulfill their individual potential – academically, socially and personally. Academy wants them to become successful, confident and caring adults, who should be able to play an active part in society where adaptability, enterprise and consideration for others have become increasingly important. Academy is affiliated to CBSE Delhi and strictly adheres to the CBSE Curriculum for its primary, middle, secondary and senior secondary classes.
